Atoms

🔬

Elements

An element contains only one type of atom.

🧩

Compounds

Compounds form when different elements chemically combine.

⚗️

Reactions

Chemical reactions transform substances into new substances.

⚛️ Understanding Atoms

The tiny world of matter

Everything around us is made of matter, and matter is built from tiny particles called atoms.


Atoms contain a nucleus with protons and neutrons, while electrons move around the nucleus.

🌡️ States of Matter

Matter commonly exists in three familiar states.

🧊 Solid Has a fixed shape and volume.
💧 Liquid Has a fixed volume but takes the container's shape.
💨 Gas Spreads out to fill its container.
