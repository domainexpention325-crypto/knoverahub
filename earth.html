🌍 What is Earth Science?

The science of our planet

Earth Science studies our planet, its structure, atmosphere, oceans, rocks, weather and the natural processes that constantly change Earth.


It helps us understand everything from earthquakes and volcanoes to clouds, oceans and Earth's history.

🌎 Layers of Earth

Earth is made of several major layers with different properties and materials.

🌱 Crust Earth's thin outer layer.
🔥 Mantle A thick layer beneath the crust.
🧲 Outer Core A liquid metallic layer.
🔴 Inner Core Earth's extremely hot solid center.

☁️ Atmosphere & Weather

☁️

Atmosphere

The atmosphere is the layer of gases surrounding Earth.

🌧️

Weather

Weather describes short-term atmospheric conditions such as temperature, rain and wind.

🌪️

Wind

Wind is moving air caused by differences in air pressure.

🪨 Rocks & Earth's Surface

🌋

Igneous Rocks

Form when molten rock cools and solidifies.

🏔️

Sedimentary Rocks

Form from deposited and compacted sediments.

💎

Metamorphic Rocks

Form when existing rocks change because of heat and pressure.
