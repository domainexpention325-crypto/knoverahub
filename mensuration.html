Mensuration

Learn how to calculate the area, perimeter and volume of different shapes and objects.

Learning Progress 85%

What is Mensuration?

Mensuration is the branch of mathematics that deals with measuring geometric figures. It helps us calculate things such as perimeter, area and volume.

Example:
If a rectangular table is 5 m long and 3 m wide, we can calculate its area using a simple formula.

Important Concepts

Perimeter

Perimeter is the total distance around a two-dimensional shape.

Rectangle = 2 × (Length + Width)

Area

Area tells us how much surface is covered inside a two-dimensional shape.

Rectangle = Length × Width

Volume

Volume tells us how much space a three-dimensional object occupies.

Cuboid = Length × Width × Height

Useful Formulas

Square

Area of a square:

Side × Side

Perimeter:

4 × Side

Rectangle

Area:

Length × Width

Perimeter:

2 × (Length + Width)

Cube

Volume:

Side × Side × Side

Let's Solve an Example

Question:

A rectangle has a length of 8 cm and a width of 5 cm. What is its area?

Area = Length × Width

Area = 8 × 5

Area = 40 cm²
