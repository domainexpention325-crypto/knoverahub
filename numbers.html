What are Numbers?

Let's start with the basic idea.

Understanding Numbers

Numbers are mathematical symbols used to represent quantities and values. We use them every day — while counting objects, measuring things, comparing quantities and solving problems.


Examples include 1, 2, 3, 10, 50, 100 and many more. Numbers can be positive, negative, whole, fractional or decimal depending on what we are representing.

Example

If you have 5 apples, the number 5 represents the quantity of apples.

Four Basic Operations

These operations are used throughout mathematics.

+

Addition

Combining quantities together.

−

Subtraction

Finding the difference between quantities.

×

Multiplication

Repeated addition of a number.

÷

Division

Splitting a quantity into equal parts.

Learn With Examples

See how basic operations work.

Addition

7 + 5 = 12

We combine 7 and 5 to get a total of 12.

Subtraction

15 − 6 = 9

Taking 6 away from 15 leaves us with 9.

Multiplication

4 × 6 = 24

6 added four times gives 24.

Division

20 ÷ 4 = 5

20 divided into 4 equal groups gives 5.
