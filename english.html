Parts of Speech

Words play different roles inside a sentence.

N

Noun

A person, place, thing or idea.

Example: teacher, school, book
P

Pronoun

A word used instead of a noun.

Example: he, she, they
V

Verb

A word that shows an action or state.

Example: run, eat, think
A

Adjective

A word that describes a noun.

Example: beautiful, tall, smart
Ad

Adverb

A word that describes a verb or adjective.

Example: quickly, slowly, very
Pr

Preposition

Shows a relationship between words.

Example: in, on, under

Build a Sentence

A simple sentence can follow this structure:

Subject + Verb + Object

The subject performs the action, the verb shows the action, and the object receives the action.

She + reads + a book.

Basic Tenses

Tenses tell us when an action happens.

1

Present

Used for actions that happen now or regularly.

She goes to school.
2

Past

Used for actions that already happened.

She went to school.
3

Future

Used for actions that will happen later.

She will go to school.
