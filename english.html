🔤 Parts of Speech

Noun

Names a person, place, thing or idea. Example: school, teacher, freedom.

Pronoun

Takes the place of a noun. Example: he, she, they, it.

Verb

Shows an action or state. Example: run, learn, is.

Adjective

Describes a noun. Example: bright, fast, beautiful.

Adverb

Gives more information about an action. Example: quickly, slowly, carefully.

Preposition

Shows a relationship between words. Example: in, on, under, beside.

🧩 Building a Sentence

Subject + Verb + Object

A simple sentence can often be understood through its subject, verb and object.

Subject: Rehaan  →  Verb: learns  →  Object: English
Complete sentence: Rehaan learns English.

⏳ Basic Tenses

🟢

Present

Used for actions happening now or regularly.

Example: I learn English.

🔵

Past

Used for actions that happened before.

Example: I learned English.

🟣

Future

Used for actions that will happen later.

Example: I will learn English.
