Ratio & Proportion

Learn how to compare quantities using ratios and understand proportional relationships.

Learning Progress 100%

What is a Ratio?

A ratio is a way of comparing two quantities. It tells us how much of one quantity there is compared with another.

Example:

Suppose there are 2 red balls and 3 blue balls.

The ratio of red balls to blue balls is:

2 : 3

Understanding Ratios

Part to Part

A ratio can compare one part with another part.

2 : 3

Part to Whole

A ratio can also compare one part with the total quantity.

2 : 5

Equivalent Ratios

Multiplying or dividing both parts by the same number gives an equivalent ratio.

2 : 3 = 4 : 6

What is Proportion?

A proportion is an equation that shows that two ratios are equal.

Example:

2 : 3 = 4 : 6

Both ratios represent the same relationship, so they form a proportion.

Finding an Unknown Value

Question:

2 : 5 = 6 : x

Since 2 becomes 6 by multiplying by 3, multiply 5 by 3 as well.

x = 15

Therefore: 2 : 5 = 6 : 15
