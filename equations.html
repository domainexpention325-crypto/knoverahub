What is a Linear Equation?

Let's understand the idea first.

A linear equation is an equation where the variable has a power of 1.

Our goal is usually to find the value of the unknown variable.

x + 5 = 12

In this equation, we need to find the value of x.

Understand the Basics

Keep both sides of an equation balanced.

Unknown

The letter represents the value we are trying to find.

x + 4 = 9

Equality

The equals sign means both sides have the same value.

5 + 3 = 8

Inverse Operations

Addition and subtraction, as well as multiplication and division, work as inverse operations.

+5 ↔ −5

Balance

Whatever operation you perform on one side should also be performed on the other.

x + 5 = 12

How to Solve an Equation

Let's solve one step by step.

1

Start with the equation

x + 5 = 12

2

Remove the +5

Subtract 5 from both sides.

3

Simplify

x = 12 − 5

4

Find the answer

x = 7

Examples

Practice recognizing the solution.

Example 01

Solve: x + 3 = 10

x = 10 − 3 = 7

Example 02

Solve: x − 4 = 9

x = 9 + 4 = 13
