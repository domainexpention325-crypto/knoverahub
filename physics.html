What is Physics?

Physics is the branch of science that studies matter, energy, motion, forces and the interactions between them.

🚲 Think About It

When a bicycle moves, its wheels rotate, forces act on it, and energy allows it to move. Physics helps us understand all of these interactions.

Core Concepts

🏃 Motion

Motion describes how an object's position changes with time.

Speed = Distance ÷ Time

💪 Force

A force is a push or pull that can change an object's motion.

Force = Mass × Acceleration

⚡ Energy

Energy is the ability to cause change or perform work.

🌍 Gravity

Gravity is the force that attracts objects toward one another.

Motion Example

Example

A car travels 100 km in 2 hours. What is its average speed?

Speed = 100 ÷ 2 = 50 km/h

Forces Around Us

Push

Applying force away from an object.

Pull

Applying force toward an object.

Friction

A force that opposes motion between surfaces.

Gravity

A force that attracts objects toward Earth.
