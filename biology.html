🧬 Core Concepts

Biology is the study of living organisms and how life works.

🔬

Cells

Cells are the basic structural and functional units of living organisms.

🫀

Human Body

The human body contains organs and systems that work together to keep us alive.

🧬

Genetics

Genetics explains how characteristics are passed from parents to offspring.

🌳

Ecosystems

Ecosystems include living organisms interacting with each other and their environment.

🔬 The Cell

The building block of life

A cell is the basic unit of life. Some organisms consist of only one cell, while others contain many cells.


Cells contain structures called organelles that perform different jobs and help the cell function properly.

🧩 Levels of Organization

In many living organisms, smaller structures combine to form larger systems.

Cell Basic unit of life
Tissue Group of similar cells
Organ Different tissues working together
Organ System Organs working together

🫀 Human Body Systems

❤️

Circulatory System

Moves blood, oxygen and nutrients throughout the body.

🫁

Respiratory System

Helps the body take in oxygen and remove carbon dioxide.

🧠

Nervous System

Helps control and coordinate activities throughout the body.
