How Science Works

01 — Observation

Scientists carefully observe the world and collect information about what they see.

02 — Question

Observations lead to questions that can be investigated scientifically.

03 — Experiment

Experiments help test ideas and collect evidence.

04 — Analysis

Scientists analyze the results and use evidence to understand what happened.
